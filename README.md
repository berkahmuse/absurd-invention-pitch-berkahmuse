## The pitch

> The Guilt Showerhead pauses your water and plays a sad violin solo whenever you linger past five minutes, so shame, not the cold, finally gets you out of the shower.

## How it works

`main.py` is a deterministic program that writes the one-sentence pitch
above to `/output/entry.txt`. The invention is the Guilt Showerhead: it
fixes the tiny everyday annoyance of lingering too long in the shower,
not by cutting the water cold, but by pausing it and playing a sad
violin solo — so shame, not the cold, finally gets you out. Nothing
else is written; the output is a single line of 165 characters
(gate: 1–220).

Run it locally (the grader sandbox writes to `/output`):

```sh
python3 main.py
```
